The driller family tree

Today's offshore drillers are the result of decades of mergers, takeovers and spin-offs. Transocean alone brings together more than twenty companies. This page shows where each driller on the rig map came from: pick one below, or scroll the timeline.

At a glance

How to read the timeline

  • Each line is a company, from its founding to today or to the deal that ended its independence (it may live on as a subsidiary).
  • Lines join where one company bought or merged with another, and branch off where a business was spun out.
  • Large labelled dots are each driller's biggest deals. Select any dot for the date, the value and the sources.
  • Point at a line to light up its whole family. Busier years are drawn wider, so check the dates along the top.
